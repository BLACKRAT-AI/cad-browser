History

Browsing history is not recorded yet. CAD Browser keeps no history database in this version, so there is nothing stored to show and nothing stored to leak.

Within a tab
Back and forward work normally: Alt+Left and Alt+Right, or the toolbar buttons.
Reopen a closed tab
Ctrl+Shift+T reopens the last 25 tabs closed in this window, most recent first.
Last session
Tabs are restored on startup when Settings > General is set to reopen the last session.
